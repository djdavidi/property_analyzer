# PWD billing and screening rules

Implemented version: `pwd-2026-09-screen-v1`, verified November 15, 2026 against the [September 2026 manual](https://water.phila.gov/wp-content/uploads/files/stormwater-credits-appeals-manual.pdf) and [effective September 1, 2026 tariff](https://water.phila.gov/wp-content/uploads/files/rates-and-charges-2026-09-01.pdf). A passing screen is not an award. Direct PDF downloads failed DNS; `data/rules/sources.json` retains those failures. The web tool read the live official PDFs; local PDFs were not obtained.

Rates, factors and thresholds are loaded from `config/rules-pwd-2026-09.json` by
`rules.load_rule_config`/`billing.default_tariff`; changing that file changes the bill
(`tests/test_policy_money.py`). Rule interpretations below were rechecked on 2026-11-16
against the original manual and tariff PDFs (SHA-256 as listed in ASSESSMENT-RULES.md).

## Calculation and ledger

One ledger (`assessment_policy._economics`) returns the baseline and the measurement-only, documented, modeled and combined variants; the report table, app and every adapter read it. The former standalone `billing.calculate_scenarios` had no production caller and was deleted (final review 2026-11-16, fix 9); its tests now exercise the ledger (`tests/test_billing.py`). Every scenario subtracts its final bill from one baseline. Independently computed savings are never added; negative differences remain negative.

Area inputs are square feet before rounding. `ga_sqft` and `ia_sqft` are physical/recorded gross and impervious area, distinct from final credit-adjusted and billed area. Existing fixed reductions are `existing_ga_credit_sqft`, `existing_ia_credit_sqft`, `existing_iar_sqft`; `npdes_fraction` records 0 or 0.07 (public `NPDESCredit`, else `ChargeSummary.NpdesCreditApplies`). `billing.recorded_credits` is the one reader of these fields for the ledger baseline and the billed-area reconciliation. A recorded `existing_iar_sqft: null` (the public record gives one impervious credit) is the labelled 'IA-side credit split unknown' case: IAR 0 and the whole impervious credit as IA credit; IAR (manual §4.3.5 step 1) and IA credit (step 2) both reduce billed IA, so the baseline bill is unchanged, while scenarios that reconcile existing credits or compute the NPDES residual are blocked with a readable reason. Missing GA/IA, credits or NPDES status produce a readable blocked reason, never a validator message or a zero. Accounts use `allocation`, `discount_factor` (1 or 0.75) and `discount_verified`. The pipeline must distinguish unverified standard-rate public calculations from account bills and must not populate missing credits or allocations as factual zero/one. Zero-credit/default-account arguments are fixture conveniences, not evidence inferences.

Tariff §§4.3(a)(2)–(6): below 5,000 GA, billing IA is 85% GA (25% for undeveloped property), even when physical measurement differs. Undeveloped status (tariff §1.0(p): BRT class SB, SC, SI, SR or SS) is three-state. The PWD record does not carry it, so `normalize_billing` stores `undeveloped: null`; `monthly_bill` refuses to choose, and the assessment ledger computes both alternatives. When recorded credits make one alternative unbillable (for example an IA credit above the 25% undeveloped statutory IA), the other is used and labelled `undeveloped_status: inferred` (basis `inference`), never silently. If both give identical charges the result is marked `unknown_immaterial`; otherwise the baseline is `unverified_alternatives` with no single total and every scenario is blocked with both alternatives attached (decision D6). Subtract IAR before management reductions; round remaining GA/IA upward to 500-square-foot increments. Rate products: GA/500 × $1.003 and IA/500 × $7.048. Nonresidential minimum SWMS is $20.89; collection is $2.81 per account. Other dates require a supplied verified tariff.

Minimum charge, one rule for baseline and every scenario (final LIVE-SAVINGS fix 9, policy version 5): manual §4.3.5 step 5 read literally — "If, after the application of all applicable credits, a parcel's monthly total SWMS charge that is calculated in Step 3 is less than the non-residential minimum monthly charge, then each account will be subject to the Non-residential minimum monthly charge" — with tariff §4.3(a)(6) ("If the monthly charge calculated in Section 4.3(a)(4) is less than the monthly charges listed below then the monthly charges below shall be billed to the Property"), §4.3(a)(8) (division among accounts, each billed Billing & Collection) and §4.5(b) (minimum after credits). The test is the PARCEL's SWMS: above $20.89 each account pays its allocated share even when that share is below $20.89; below it each account (with a nonzero allocation) is billed $20.89, then $2.81. Evidence (2026-11-16 snapshot, `data/pwd-snapshot/raw`, 2,590 parcels): 7 multi-account nonresidential parcels distinguish this reading from the former per-account reading (`max(share, $20.89)`, from the PWD billing FAQ) — 191080, 206992, 475061, 496856, 531167, 539895, 905771 — and PWD's published totals follow the literal reading on all 7 (0 follow the per-account reading; e.g. 9414 Meadowbrook Ave: four $17.84 shares billed, $82.60, not $94.80). No snapshot parcel has several accounts below the minimum, so "each account" versus "billed to the Property" in that case is unverified; the bill carries a `billing_notes` line saying so. Consequence: a credit that pushes a multi-account parcel below the minimum can raise the bill (three accounts: $34.56 → $71.10); that scenario carries a `manual_rule_discontinuity` note (`billing.minimum_discontinuity`) instead of being hidden. An account with a 0% allocation (approved Form A-1 reallocation) is billed no SWMS share and no Billing & Collection charge, as PWD bills all 60 such accounts (23 parcels) in the snapshot.

Account allocation: tariff §4.3(a)(8) and manual §2.2.6 divide SWMS equally unless PWD approved a Form A-1 reallocation. A missing public allocation stays `null` in the baseline. Billing then shows the equal-division default as a labelled, unverified alternative (`allocation_status`), and sets `allocation_sensitive` when the parcel minimum binds on several accounts, because the unknown actual allocation (a 0% account is billed nothing) could change the total. A partly known allocation is not completed by assumption.

Billed-area validation (review G1): `billed_ga_sqft`/`billed_ia_sqft` (PWD `FinalGross`/`FinalImperv`) are compared with the computed final and 500 sq ft billed areas. A value matching neither is a visible `billed_area_check` mismatch, a warning and an assessment review flag; it never corrects anything.

Official texts do not prescribe intermediate cent rounding. PWD's published calculation rounds each money step in binary floating point, scaled by 100 and rounded half to even (the .NET `Math.Round(double, 2)` default): GA charge = round(GA units × 1.003), IA charge likewise, parcel SWMS = round(GA + IA charge), each account share = round(SWMS × AccountPercentOfCharge), account total = round((share + $2.81) × discount). `billing.pwd_cents` reproduces it (fix 10). Reconciliation over the snapshot (`normalize_billing` → `recorded_credits` → `monthly_bill` against the sum of current `TotalWeightedCharge`): before, Decimal `ROUND_HALF_UP` with a per-account minimum matched 2,056 of 2,325 computable nonresidential/condominium parcels (269 off: 240 by $0.01–$0.36 and 29 by $1 or more, from rounding, the minimum reading and 0% accounts); after, 2,325 of 2,325 match to the cent. All 6,071 current-year account rows match GrossCharge, ImpervCharge and AccountSubtotal, and the 5,983 rows outside flat-rate residential parcels match DistributedAccountSubtotal; exact-decimal half-up misses 119 GA-charge ties (e.g. 3060 S 61st St: 1,315 × $1.003 = $1,318.945 → PWD $1,318.94, published $9,137.98) and 3000 Walnut St's $4,696.815 account share (binary 4,696.81499… → $4,696.81, published $9,399.24). Not computed: 15 parcels with NPDES plus recorded credits (blocked by design), 4 without accounts, 246 residential/exempt/cemetery types. The 75% special-rate rounding is not observable in the snapshot and uses the same rule (unverified). `tests/fixtures/billing/pwd-snapshot-rounding-minimum-2026-11-16.json` freezes six of these parcels. This is not proof of payment. Verified 75% special rates apply after credits to the entire bill, including collection, per manual §4.3.5. No enrollment is inferred from property use.

## Overlap and evidence

Benefits require disjoint physical `ledger_ids`. Repeated IDs, missing IDs for multiple passing benefits, excess physical allocations, unreconciled existing-credit overlap, or credits exceeding corrected area block the affected scenario. For canopy plus management, first resolve the overlap into disjoint remaining accounting areas. The module does not invent priority.

Existing aggregate credits need `existing_credits_spatially_reconciled=true` and an itemized approved allocation ledger before changes; each new benefit needs `existing_overlap_reconciled=true`. These flags record a supported review decision.

NPDES has one representation: area credits (manual §4.3.5.2; tariff §4.5(e) delegates the application of credits to the manual). `monthly_bill` has no charge-percentage discount. A recorded NPDES credit with no other recorded credit is "7% credit towards both the IA and GA square footage" in the baseline, identical to a screened permit record (the same mixed site was $126.34 by charge discount and $127.59 by area credit; both are now $127.59). A recorded NPDES credit together with recorded GA/IA credits blocks the baseline with a readable reason: whether the recorded credits already include the 7% is unknown. New benefits under a recorded NPDES credit are priced with the 87%/97% factors and the residual rule in ASSESSMENT-RULES.md.

`screen(category, area_sqft, evidence)` keeps missing and failed prerequisites separate. Missing inputs produce null supported credit, not zero eligible area. Potential formula areas remain visible but are excluded from supported savings. All screens require confirmed physical area, parcel allocation, and current account standing. Explicit assumptions may be supplied with benefit `basis='modeled'`; they remain separate from `basis='documented'`.

- Canopy: half of confirmed overlap with ground-level IA (manual §4.3.2.1). Roofs/lawn do not qualify. Physical IA is unchanged; this IAR affects IA only, with no automatic GA reduction.
- Roof disconnection: pervious receiving dimensions at least contributing dimensions; ≤500 sq ft/downspout; continuous unique route without DCIA; ≥15 ft from ground IA; receiving slope >0 and ≤5%; non-D soil. Dimension confirmation must come from measurement.
- Pavement disconnection: adequate pervious receiving dimensions, vegetated area, impervious flow path ≤75 ft, contributing slope ≤5%, receiving slope >0 and ≤5%, non-D soil, and sheet flow. Concentrated discharge needs ≤1,000 sq ft/point and erosion control.
- Open space: confirmed non-impervious cover, soil and runoff evidence with average NRCS CN. Table A-2 gives 4% per whole CN below 75, capped at 80% for CN ≤55; the existing-NPDES column uses 4.35% and 87%. Appendix A steps 3–5 define the average as Σ(area × CN) / Σ area; it is summed in Decimal so a uniform CN61 split into strips stays exactly 61 (review B4). Manual §4.3.3.1 applies "a percent reduction ... for each whole number below a CN of 75", so a fractional average counts whole steps only (61.4 → 13 steps → 52%); PWD has not confirmed fractional handling. Table A-1 matches the manual exactly, including brush = woods-grass and porous turf 70/70/79/84/69 (review B5 withdrawn).
- Managed IA: route, accepted drawings, depth ≥1.5 inches, maintained/current condition and professional review. Verified old-standard renewal can use 1 inch; project dates and Expired flags cannot establish that exception. Standard non-surface management gives 80% IA and equivalent GA underneath.
- Green roof: manual Table 1 lists "Rooftop Disconnection via Green Roof" under Impervious Area Reductions, and §4.3.2.1 states "IARs result in a direct reduction of the billable IA" (tariff §4.5(c)(1)(i)(A), rooftop disconnection). The IAR is the green-roof footprint on mapped physical IA (no GA credit, no 80% factor); its contributing area is not credited by the IAR. Manual §4.5.1 (Impervious Area Reductions): "a site plan delineating the requested areas to be credited as well as recent photographs" (`site_plan_documented`, `photographs_current`), plus the common account/area/installation facts.
